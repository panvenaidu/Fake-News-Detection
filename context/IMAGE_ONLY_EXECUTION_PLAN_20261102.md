| Setting | Proposed production contract |
|---|---|

| Images | Strict Pillow decode and RGB conversion. Per-ID/epoch/seed random resized crop to 224, area fraction 0.8–1.0 and aspect ratio 0.75–4/3, bilinear antialiasing. Keep the validated transforms and avoid horizontal flips so text inside an image stays legible. |
| Optimization | Unweighted six-class cross-entropy on logits. AdamW, backbone and head LR 1e-4, weight decay 1e-4 on weight matrices/convolutions, zero on bias and normalization, betas (0.9, 0.999), epsilon 1e-8, global norm clip 1.0. No resampling. |

| Precision and order | Single CUDA device, FP16 AMP with FP32 parameters and FP32 CE/softmax for evaluation.
