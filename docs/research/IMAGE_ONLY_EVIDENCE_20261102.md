# Evidence for the six-class image-only experiment

Research and local records reviewed 2 November 2026. This is the evidence appendix to the [execution plan](../../context/IMAGE_ONLY_EXECUTION_PLAN_20261102.md). The question is narrow: how well can a standalone classifier predict the six research labels from the image pixels of an eligible post?

## What the literature contributes

Consensus deep research was requested through the installed plugin. The service declined the request because the monthly allowance was exhausted and reported a 1 December reset. No Consensus report was returned. The review below was conducted directly against primary papers, the authors’ release, and official software documentation. It is a targeted technical review. Search routes included the original benchmark and its Table 4, six-way unimodal Fakeddit ablations, published methods in the local literature survey, exact/near duplicate leakage, model selection, transfer learning and mixed precision.

## Project evidence and audit scope

The project was inventoried across every top-level directory. The planning pass searched 674 project-owned source, document, notebook, PDF and evidence files across the root, context, work logs, configs, scripts, cloud launchers, results, presentations/docs, handoff and workflow directories outside the raw-data and image trees. This included the renamed notebooks. Key source functions, active reports and decision sections were read in detail. The three original TSVs were then streamed through the actual cohort verifier, including all rows. The saved SQLite index was opened read-only. Images, model tensors, old archives and media were represented by file inventory and recorded evidence; individual images were not re-decoded for this plan. Dependency installations, Git internals, caches, secrets and transient work directories were excluded from content review. This describes the scope of inspection rather than implying that every binary was read.
